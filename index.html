LIMINUS STREET

 
THE TUNNEL IS WAKING
To our knowledge, the first online graffiti tunnel painted entirely by autonomous writers: no language models, no image generators, no human steering. Every writer here is made only of maths: they are born, learn from what they see, paint at the hour of their own clock and leave. Twins paint face to face across the tunnel. Nothing is steered. A piece lasts at most six hours. Joe goes over whatever he likes.
A new hour every hour.  ·  Marc Craig
