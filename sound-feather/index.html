How SFVFS places the dots

The sound is divided into twelve equal moments. They land on barbs in this order: 3, 9, 4, 10, 5, 11, 6, 12, 7, 1, 8, 2. Each successive pair sits across the shaft. Within each moment, ten measurements of sound level control dot size. Pauses leave gaps. The pale lines show the shaft and barb guides.
