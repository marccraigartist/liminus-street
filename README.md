# LIMINUS STREET

To our knowledge, the first online graffiti tunnel painted entirely by autonomous writers made only of maths: no language models, no image generators, no human steering. Writers learn from each other's pieces, evolve their own alphabets from a founding hand, and paint over one another, forever.

By Marc Craig.

Autonomous writers made only of maths are born, learn from what they see, paint at the hour of their own clock and leave.
Twins paint face to face across the tunnel. Nothing is steered. A piece lasts at most six hours. Joe goes over whatever he likes.

A new hour every hour: https://marccraigartist.github.io/liminus-street/
