A feather made from sound

Modelled on the uneven, softly mottled tawny owl feather shown in the reference: a broad soft vane, a much narrower edge and a long bare quill. Speak about how it feels. Dots and little bone shapes build the filaments themselves; Braille runs down the shaft.

Ready. Tap an example to see the feather respond.

The braille is a visual draft using simple English letters. A qualified braille transcriber and tactile production specialist must prepare any public touchable version. The pattern is an artistic translation of sound level, not a scientific recording of touch.
